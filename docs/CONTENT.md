# Content and sources

| Dhikr | Benefit shown in the app | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Subhanallah x100 | equals freeing 100 slaves from the children of Isma'il; 1000 good deeds | Umm Hani's hadith (Ahmad, Ibn Majah; grading discussed); Muslim 2698 |
| Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi x100 | sins forgiven even if like the foam of the sea | Bukhari 6405; Muslim 2691 |
| Alhamdulillah x100 | equals 100 horses ridden in the path of Allah; fills the scale | Umm Hani's hadith; Muslim 223 |
| La ilaha illallah x100 | fills what is between sky and earth | Umm Hani's hadith |
| La ilaha illallahu wahdahu... x100 | equals freeing ten slaves, 100 good deeds, 100 sins erased, protection from Shaytan | Bukhari 3293; Muslim 2691 |
| Allahu Akbar x100 | equals 100 camels offered in sacrifice and accepted | Umm Hani's hadith |
| La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah | treasure from the treasures of Paradise | Bukhari 4205; Muslim 2704 |
| La hawla (99 diseases wording) | cure for 99 diseases, the least being worry | Tabarani, weak chain per many scholars |
| Darood | 10 blessings, 10 sins forgiven, 10 ranks raised, 10 good deeds | Nasa'i 1297; Ahmad; Muslim 408 |
| Jazallahu anna Muhammadan | dua of gratitude to the Prophet ﷺ | Tabarani, grading discussed |
| Astaghfirullah | the Prophet ﷺ sought forgiveness 70 to 100 times a day | Bukhari 6307; Muslim 2702; Qur'an 71:10-12 |
| Astaghfirullah al-'Azeem alladhi la ilaha illa huwa... | forgiven even if he fled from the battlefield | Abu Dawud 1517; Tirmidhi 3577 |

Note on the "foam of the sea" promise: it is reported for **Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi x100**, not for istighfar, so the app places it there.

Please verify hadith numbers and gradings with your teacher. Use the in-app admin mode to adjust any text.
